THE JOURNEY / CHAPTER 04

Go somewhere
unforgettable.

Palaces, colorful neighborhoods, island landscapes, and ancient sites. Here are four wonderful places to begin exploring South Korea.

Four places to visit in South Korea

01

THE CAPITAL / CITY & HERITAGE

Seoul

See the changing of the seasons around Gyeongbokgung Palace, discover the narrow streets of traditional neighborhoods, and experience the energy of a modern capital.

DON'T MISS: Gyeongbokgung Palace & Bukchon Hanok Village

Explore Seoul ↗
02

THE COAST / ART & OCEAN

Busan

Head south for seaside views, beaches, and colorful neighborhoods. Gamcheon Culture Village is particularly known for its hillside homes and artwork.

DON'T MISS: Gamcheon Culture Village & Haeundae Beach

Learn more ↗
03

THE ISLAND / NATURE & SLOW DAYS

Jeju Island

For a change of pace, explore volcanic scenery, striking coastlines, waterfalls, and peaceful landscapes. Jeju is a favorite for nature-focused travelers.

DON'T MISS: Seongsan Ilchulbong & coastal views

Learn more ↗
04

THE HERITAGE / HISTORY & REFLECTION

Gyeongju

Once the capital of the Silla Kingdom, Gyeongju brings Korean history to life with ancient royal tombs, temples, and beautiful historic grounds.

DON'T MISS: Bulguksa Temple & Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond

MAKE IT YOUR OWN

What kind of
traveler are you?

There is no single right way to explore Korea. Choose a destination that matches the experience you want most.

  1. Love a busy city? Start with Seoul.
  2. Want ocean views and colorful streets? Try Busan.
  3. Prefer nature and open space? Visit Jeju.
  4. Inspired by ancient history? Explore Gyeongju.

Check the official Korea Tourism Organization website for practical details and current travel information before your trip.
