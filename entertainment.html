THE MUSIC / 01

More than
a catchy song.

K-pop blends music, choreography, performance, and a strong connection between artists and fans. Concerts, music videos, and fan communities help make it a global phenomenon.

Artists such as BTS, BLACKPINK, and LE SSERAFIM are examples of Korean pop acts known to international listeners.

THE STORIES / 02

Just one more
episode.

Korean dramas are known for memorable characters, emotional storytelling, romance, comedy, and suspense. For some viewers, watching a series is what first sparks an interest in Korea.

Popular examples include Crash Landing on You and Extraordinary Attorney Woo.
